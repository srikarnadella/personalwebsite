# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers at tech companies and financial institutions. They open many portfolio tabs in a single session and decide in 4–5 seconds whether to stay. They want to see who this person is, what they've shipped, and whether the taste is right — fast.

## Product Purpose

Srikar Nadella's personal portfolio. Converts recruiter attention into contact or interview requests. Success is measured by whether a recruiter scrolls past the hero and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Technically sharp, warm, unapologetic. Not corporate, not shy about the orange. Direct.

## Anti-references

- Minimalist white resume sites: all-white, Helvetica, centered name/title.
- Template-y portfolios: "Hi, I'm [Name], I build things" with icon grid of skills.
- Beige / muted-slate safe-mode portfolios.
- Generic dark SaaS with purple gradient blobs.

## Design Principles

1. **Clear in 5 seconds.** The name, role, and most impressive credential must be readable at a glance — no hunting through DL grids or numbered blocks.
2. **Own the orange.** The brand color should do structural work, not just accent detail. Committed or Drenched, not Restrained.
3. **Don't bury the lead.** Experience and school go near the name, not after two dividers and a label grid.
4. **Remove ceremony, keep substance.** No numbered section markers, no horizontal-rule-as-punctuation, no intro copy that restates the heading.
5. **Distinguish or be ignored.** An average dark portfolio is invisible. Every design choice should be answerable with "because it's Srikar's site," not "because it looks professional."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Dark theme throughout. Key text (name, role, experience) must meet WCAG AA contrast on the dark background. Reduce-motion consideration: decorative canvas animations should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
